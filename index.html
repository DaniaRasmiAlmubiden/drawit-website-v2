FAQ

Questions parents usually ask.

Need something else? Reach us at info@drawit.kids or WhatsApp us.

What age is Drawit designed for?

Drawit is designed for children ages 4–12.

How much is delivery?

A flat 3 JOD anywhere in Jordan — and completely free when you order 3 or more kits.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. You can pay cash when your kit arrives, or pay online securely by card.

Is Drawit available outside Jordan?

International orders are handled through our priority waiting list while we expand availability — join it and we'll contact you the moment we deliver to your country.

What's the return policy?

You can return an unused kit in its original condition within 7 days of delivery. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll take care of it.

Does Drawit diagnose mental-health conditions?

No. Drawit provides supportive insights and does not replace qualified medical or mental-health professionals.
